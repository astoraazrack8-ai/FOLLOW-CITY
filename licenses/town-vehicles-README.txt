Town Vehicles
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/town-vehicles

A free low-poly car and vehicle kit for Roblox Studio: 24 matching GLB models for a roleplay town, a driving game or a car dealership. Everyday cars include a silver family sedan, a family SUV and a pearl white minivan, with a red wedge sports car, a baby blue convertible and a stretch limousine for something flashier. Work vehicles cover a blue pickup truck, a delivery van, a garbage truck, a tow truck and a pastel ice cream truck. For fun there is a monster truck, an orange go-kart and a lime dirt bike, and a yellow rescue helicopter takes to the air. Bright, simple colours keep each one easy to spot on a busy street. Park them along the kerb, line them up in a showroom, or hand them out as job vehicles. Wheels and doors are separate parts, ready for you to wire up for driving with your own scripts.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
