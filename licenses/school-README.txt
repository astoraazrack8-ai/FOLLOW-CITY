School
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/school

A free low-poly school kit for Roblox Studio: 27 matching GLB models for a school roleplay, a campus hangout or a classroom mini-game. A red brick school building and a yellow school bus set the scene outside. Inside, rooms include a classroom set, a cafeteria set, a gym set, a science lab set and a library set. An auditorium stage, a principal's office set and a nurse office give roleplay its key locations, and rusty school lockers line the hallways. There are enough rooms to give every player a timetable, from art and music to the computer lab, so the school day never runs out of places to be. Teachers, students and staff can all find a role, from the front office to the last bell. The models are free to use in your own games, so a school roleplay can start from this one kit and grow from there.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
