City
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/city

A free low-poly city kit for Roblox Studio: 25 matching GLB models for an urban block, a city roleplay or a busy downtown map. Buildings like a brick city apartment, a corner shop, a city cafe and a brick fire station line the streets, with a subway entrance leading below. Vehicles include a city bus, a taxi cab, a ladder fire truck and a food truck. Street life comes from street market tents, a hot dog cart and a red phone booth. A basketball half-court and a playground set give players places to hang out, and a scaffolding kit shows a city still under construction. Put it together as a single dense block, or spread it out along the roads from the Town Streets kit. Sizes are in studs, so the bus, the taxi and the apartments come in at a matching scale with the rest of the library.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
