Hospital
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/hospital

A free low-poly hospital kit for Roblox Studio: 21 matching GLB models for a hospital roleplay, a town medical centre or a doctor and nurse game. The hospital building comes with a rooftop helipad and an ambulance for emergency arrivals. Inside, a reception desk and a waiting room lead to an emergency room bay, a modern operating room and a patient room. Specialist spaces like an X-ray room, a dentist office, a baby ward and a physical therapy room give players more jobs to take on, and a pharmacy counter handles prescriptions on the way out. A vet clinic adds a place for animal patients. Hospital signage helps players find their way between departments. With this many rooms, doctors, nurses and patients each have somewhere to be. Every model is static and free of scripts, so admissions, treatments and shifts can follow whatever roleplay systems you build.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
