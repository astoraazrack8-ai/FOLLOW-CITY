Police & Fire
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/police-and-fire

A free low-poly police and fire station kit for Roblox Studio: 28 matching GLB models for an emergency services roleplay, a cops and robbers game or a busy town map. The police side starts with a town police station, a holding cell and an interrogation room, plus an evidence room for detective work. Patrol vehicles include a patrol car, a police motorcycle and a police helicopter, and a police dog joins the team. The fire side has a fire station interior, a fire training tower, a dispatch desk and a firefighter gear set, with a brush fire truck and a fire chief SUV ready to respond. A police roadblock set brings the work out onto the streets. Players can split into officers and firefighters and still cover the same town. Vehicles and buildings share one stylised low-poly look, so the station, the patrol car and the fire truck sit naturally on the same street.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
