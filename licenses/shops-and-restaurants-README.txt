Shops & Restaurants
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/shops-and-restaurants

A free low-poly shops and restaurants kit for Roblox Studio: 44 matching GLB models for a roleplay town high street, a shopping mall or a job-based work game. Food spots include a retro burger drive-in, a retro diner, a conveyor sushi bar, an ice cream parlour and a bubble tea shop. Shops range from a clothing boutique interior to a pet shop and a supermarket checkout. Services like a hair salon, a bank counter and a post office counter give players more jobs to take, while a classic cinema front, a bowling alley and a hotel lobby add places to spend a night out. A mall atrium ties the indoor shops together. With over 40 places to work and shop, there is room for plenty of players to run a business each. Signs are left blank for your own names, and every model is free to use in your own games.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
