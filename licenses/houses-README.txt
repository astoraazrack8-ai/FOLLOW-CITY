Houses
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/houses

A free low-poly house kit for Roblox Studio: 45 matching GLB models for a roleplay town, a family life game or a suburban neighbourhood map. Homes range from a cosy starter house and a ranch bungalow to a suburban family house, a modern flat-roof villa and a grand white mansion. Backyards fill out with a pool house, a hot tub, a fire pit set and a doghouse yard set. Over 20 room sets cover the inside of a home, including a modern kitchen set, a cosy living room set, a pastel nursery set, a teen gaming bedroom and a cosy home theatre. Each room set groups the furniture for one space, so you can furnish a house a room at a time. Mix the different homes along a street for a neighbourhood that does not repeat itself. Houses, yards and rooms all share one stylised look and are sized in studs, so a whole street of homes reads as one place.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
