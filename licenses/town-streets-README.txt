Town Streets & Park
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/town-streets

A free low-poly town streets and park kit for Roblox Studio: 32 matching GLB models for the roads, civic buildings and green spaces of a roleplay town or city map. A road kit, a sidewalk kit and a parking lot kit form the base, with a roundabout island, a bus stop and a streetlight set along the way. Civic buildings like a town hall, a courthouse and a modern public library give the centre its landmarks. The park side has a park gazebo, a dog park, a tennis court and a skate park, so there is always somewhere to hang out between jobs. Use it to join houses, shops and services into one connected town, with a welcome sign at the edge to greet new arrivals. Sizes are in studs, so road, sidewalk and parking pieces come in at the same scale as the houses, shops and vehicles from the other town kits.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
